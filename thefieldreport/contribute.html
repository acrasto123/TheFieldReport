Contribute a Brief

Open to all NYU School of Public Health students. Bring your country, your concentration, and a paper you found interesting.

What we're looking for

We publish one brief every two weeks, rotating across countries and concentrations. We are especially interested in papers from countries underrepresented in US public health literature.

You will need:

  • A peer-reviewed paper in any public health area
  • 400–600 words of clear, non-specialist writing
  • One visualization or tool output
  • A connection to your concentration
  • A brief "US context" paragraph at the end

The process

Submit your interest below. The editor will confirm your slot, connect you with a faculty reviewer in your department, and guide you through submission. Most briefs take 2–3 hours to write once you have your paper.


What you get

Your brief is published on The Field Report with your name, program, and faculty reviewer credited. It is a real, faculty-reviewed writing sample you can use in job applications and graduate school admissions — something most students leave school without.
