About The Field Report

A student-led initiative bridging the gap between classroom knowledge and global public health practice.

The problem we're solving

Public health students graduate with academic knowledge but few applied writing samples, limited experience with real-world tools, and a curriculum that is overwhelmingly American in its focus — despite working in a global field. Most students complete their degree without a single published piece of professional writing.

How it works

Every two weeks, one student selects a peer-reviewed paper from a country of their choosing, connects it to their concentration, and translates the findings for a non-specialist audience using a job-relevant tool — Power BI, Tableau, GIS, R, and more. A faculty member reviews the draft before publication.

Why the global map

The interactive map on our homepage shows every country a brief has been written about. It is a live record of the platform's global reach — and a signal of where we still need to go. Every empty region is an invitation for the next writer.

What writers get out of it

A published, faculty-reviewed writing sample attributed to them by name. Hands-on experience with tools employers actually use. A global perspective that goes beyond US-centric public health education. And something real to show when applying for jobs or graduate programs.
